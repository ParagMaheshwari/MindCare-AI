Wellness Tips

General, educational guidance — not a treatment plan or medical advice.

Better Sleep

Try keeping a consistent sleep and wake time, even on weekends, and wind down screens before bed to help your body settle into rest.

Digital Wellbeing

Set app time limits, turn off non-essential notifications, and build in regular screen-free breaks during the day.

Stress Management

Short breathing exercises, brief walks, and scheduled breaks can help you reset during stressful periods. Talk to someone you trust if stress feels hard to manage.

Study-Life Balance

Try Pomodoro-style sessions — focused study blocks followed by short breaks — to stay productive without burning out.

Physical Activity

Regular movement — walking, stretching, or sports — supports both physical and mental wellbeing, even in short daily sessions.

Social Connection

Make time for the people who support you. Real conversations and shared activities can be as restorative as rest itself.

These tips are general and educational. They are not medical advice and do not replace guidance from a qualified professional.
