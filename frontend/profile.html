Privacy Controls & Data Management

MindCare AI stores your assessment history, journal reflections, and mood check-ins privately in your local browser storage. You can delete individual categories or completely reset your data at any time.

Clear Assessment History
Deletes all past machine-learning prediction scores and input responses.
Clear Journal Entries & AI Reflections
Permanently removes all written journal thoughts and AI companion reflections.
Clear Mood History
Removes all daily mood check-ins and resets your mood streak.
Clear Chatbot Session
Clears current and cached AI conversation history.
Delete All Local Account Data
Completely wipes all assessments, journals, moods, and profile settings for this account.
